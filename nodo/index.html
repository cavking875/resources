WELCOME TO NODO

Little shapes.
Big satisfaction.

Your aim is simple: fill every empty space without overlapping the pieces.

  1. Choose a puzzle and a difficulty.
  2. Pick a piece from the colourful tray.
  3. Use the rotate, flip and tilt buttons to change its orientation.
  4. Tap an open sphere position to snap your selected piece into place.
  5. Drag the board to orbit, zoom to inspect hidden layers, and tap a placed piece to remove it.
  6. Fill every space to win. Hints highlight one possible piece location.

Each layout has its own original set of connected shapes; these are not replicas of the commercial Kanoodle pieces.
