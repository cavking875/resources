Guide

How to play NODO

Fill every empty sphere position using all the coloured connected-ball pieces. Pieces must fit inside the outline and must not overlap.

  1. Pick a layout and difficulty from the left.
  2. Choose a coloured piece from the tray (above the board on mobile).
  3. Rotate, flip, or tilt it, then tap a vacant position on the 3D board to snap it into place.
  4. Drag the board to see its sides and hidden layers. Use your mouse wheel or pinch to zoom.
  5. Tap an already placed piece in the tray to take it back. Use Hint to show a suggested location.
  6. Fill every slot to complete the puzzle. Locked starter pieces cannot be moved.

All layouts are constructed from valid arrangements, including the 55-sphere five-level pyramid.
